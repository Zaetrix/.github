## ABOUT US

Zaetrix is a small-ish group of coders who build tools that we actually need. No frills, no over-engineering, just code that does what it’s supposed to do. We don’t care about impressing anyone. If it helps us, we share it. If it helps you too, even better.

We’re all about building simple, straightforward software that gets the job done—whether that’s automating a task, streamlining a workflow, or just solving a small problem we encountered along the way. We believe in practicality over perfection, and sometimes that means cutting out the fluff and focusing on what really matters.

Our projects are built from scratch (no complex frameworks unless we absolutely need them) and usually come with just the core functionality to get things done. It’s all about doing the work we find interesting and helpful, and if we can share it with others, we do.

## WHAT WE DO

At Zaetrix, we work on a variety of projects—everything from tiny utilities to larger, more complex tools. Here are some examples of the types of things we like to build:

    Developer Tools: We love building things that make life easier for developers, from small CLIs to integrations with the tools we use every day.

    Automation Scripts: Whether it's automating tasks or building smart systems, we like to streamline our workflows with code.

    Open-Source Libraries: We create and share simple libraries that solve real problems—no unnecessary features or bloat.

    Experimental Projects: Sometimes, we just build things because we’re curious. These are often side projects or fun experiments that might turn into something useful or just teach us something new.

## WHY WE DO IT

We’re not here to push the boundaries of tech or make a name for ourselves. We’re just a bunch of coders who enjoy the process of building things. The goal is simple: to make software that works. If we learn something in the process or if others find our tools helpful, that’s the bonus.

Sometimes, we tackle bigger projects or dive into something more complex, but it’s always driven by what interests us and the problems we’re trying to solve. No strict deadlines, no corporate pressure, just code for the sake of code.

## GET INVOLVED

If you like what we’re building, feel free to get involved! Whether it’s submitting a bug report, offering a pull request, or just letting us know how one of our tools helped you out, we love hearing from other people who share our values of simplicity and functionality.

    Contribute: Check out our open issues or start your own project if you have an idea you think fits.

    Feedback: If you’ve used one of our tools, let us know what works and what doesn’t. We’re always open to suggestions.

    Collaborate: If you’re working on something similar or just want to team up on a fun project, feel free to reach out!

## HOW TO USE OUR TOOLS

Our tools are generally designed to be as simple as possible. Each project comes with clear instructions on how to install, configure, and use it. Here’s how you can get started:

    Clone or Download the repository.

    Install any dependencies (usually listed in the README or requirements.txt file).

    Run the tool and start experimenting!
